How to play: Roll a 6 to bring a token out. After rolling, select one of the current player's tokens to move it around the demo track. First player to reach 20 steps with a token wins. Local pass-and-play only.

This is a simplified Ludo-inspired UI prototype for a student hackathon, not a complete official Ludo rules engine.
